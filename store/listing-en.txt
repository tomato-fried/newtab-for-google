Replaces the new tab page with Google.
When you open a new tab, the cursor goes to the Google search box, not the address bar, so you can start typing right away.

Manifest V3.
No permissions, no JavaScript. This extension does not collect any data.
The code is one HTML file, so there is almost nothing to break.

Your browser may ask if you want to keep the new tab change after install. Choose "Keep changes".

Not affiliated with Google.
